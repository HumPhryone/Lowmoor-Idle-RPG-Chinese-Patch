The bestiary plates are the work of six artists and are used here as
        adaptations: each drawing was converted to greyscale and reduced to line
        for the plate frame, and nothing else about it was altered.
        The two module covers and the plates of the bone-dog and the leech are
        adapted the same way from work that is out of copyright by age: Harry
        Clarke's illustrations to Poe's Tales of Mystery and Imagination (1919)
        and to Faust (1925), and Thomas Bewick's wood engravings for A General
        History of Quadrupeds (1790). Clarke died in 1931 and Bewick in 1828,
        and no licence is claimed over either.
